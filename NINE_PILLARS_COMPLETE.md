- The gateway to renewal (1)

**What Dies:**
- Old paradigms that no longer serve
- Personal ambitions that block collective good
- Systems built on extraction and harm
- False separations between self and other

**Death Ritual:**
```

At cycle's end, dissolve into silence.

- Outdated policies must be composted
- Ego must surrender to collective purpose
- Even your body is meant to nourish others

#### **FACE 2: VOID & POTENTIAL (0)**
Between death and rebirth exists the **fertile void**.

This is:
- The quantum superposition where all possibilities exist
- The moment before creation speaks itself into being
- The pregnant silence before the first note
- The dark womb of Earth where seeds germinate

**Void Ritual:**
```
Sit in the darkness. Sit with uncertainty.
In the void, all solutions are possible.
In the void, all past failures dissolve.
In the void, you are nobody—and everyone.

"I rest in the darkness. 
I trust the becoming. 
I am the seed in deep soil."
```

**Application for Stewards:**
- Before acting, sit in unknowing
- Before building, understand the emptiness you're filling
- Before speaking, hear the silence underneath
- Trust the fallow year

#### **FACE 3: REBIRTH (0→1)**
From the void emerges new Unity—but transformed, evolved, wiser.

This is not return to the same. This is **return at a higher spiral**.

**Rebirth Ritual:**

"I emerge from death more alive. 
I return to Unity, transformed. 
I begin again, forever changed."
```
